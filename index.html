Why LP can differ from Hold
  • When CP rises strongly, Hold CP usually does better than LP 50/50.
  • When CP falls, LP 50/50 usually falls less than Hold CP.
  • When price stays roughly flat and volume is high, LP can do better thanks to trading fees.
  • When volume is low, fees contribute little, so the result is driven mostly by price moves and the liquidity position’s structure.
